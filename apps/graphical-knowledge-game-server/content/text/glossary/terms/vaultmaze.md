VaultMaze is the composable knowledge-navigation and learning application that
must meet and beat Microsoft MindMaze. It forms admitted CAIRN resources into
rooms, passages, review experiences, and user-configured routes without
becoming their content repository. The engineering realization is the
[VaultMaze application](../../../../README.md).

## VaultMaze as FeltOS and GEODE

VaultMaze is to grow into FeltOS combined with GEODE: the server that lets
agents *play* the knowledge, as Encarta's MindMaze let its players walk an
encyclopedia, improved by what pedagogy and the learning sciences have learned
since (Leader, 2026-09-25). Turntide Island patrons reach L-Space as a VaultMaze
interface (2026-09-23 ecosystem note).
