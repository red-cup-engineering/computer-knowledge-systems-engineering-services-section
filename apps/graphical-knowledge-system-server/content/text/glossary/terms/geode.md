GEODE means **Graphical Entity Ontology Difference Engine**. It is the
graphical workbench for inspecting and acting on differences among entity
organizations, assertions, relationships, projections, and revisions. GEODE
composes CAIRN content-archive capabilities into a VICTOR website whose
user-facing forms and topology are supplied by TUFTE. The engineering
realization is the [GEODE application](../../../../README.md).

## GEODE against Obsidian

GEODE is to meet and then beat Obsidian's capabilities. It provides them
through a web browser user interface, and it also provides an API through
which agents work the same knowledge: rewriting notes, rewriting links,
graphing, and searching by meaning (Leader, 2026-09-25).

GEODE is the durable tooling for condensing the house's knowledge: knowledge
scattered across too many notes, too few glossary terms, and too poor
hyperlinking is settled from fuzzy, opaque notes toward machine-readable,
determined, clean information. Many notes say the same thing in slightly
different ways as the Group learned a concept; GEODE is how those notes find
each other. The registers of nomenclature, and their relation to formal concept
analysis, are part of what it must understand (see
`2026-07-04-nomenclature-registers-as-forced-readouts.md` in the emsenn
section).

The knowledge GEODE serves lies in this repository, on Cold Red Gate, and in
the Group's GitHub repositories. L-Space is meant to be reachable as a GEODE
frontend for the Leader (2026-09-23 ecosystem note).
