CAIRN means **Content Archive Inspection, Revision, and Navigation**. It is the
headless application boundary for admitted digital content archives: addresses,
metadata, relationships, readings, queries, revisions, and receipts. Markdown
is its first discrete adapter, not the limit of the term. The engineering
realization is the [CAIRN application](../../../../README.md).

CAIRN is the knowledge-vault command-line engine (the Leader, 2026-10-06): its
command application and JavaScript API are how an agent or person works a
vault. It holds no SQLite store of its own; the admitted archive is its store
(the Leader, 2026-09-25). See
[the consolidation directives of September 2026](../../../../../../../../../../../../content/text/documents/articles/notes/2026/10/10/2026-10-10-note-on-the-consolidation-directives-of-september-2026.md).
