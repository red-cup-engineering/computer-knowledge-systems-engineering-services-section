VICTOR means **Voxelized Information Catalog Topos Orienting Relations**. It is
the website framework that forms domain applications and authored resources
into complete, conformant, buildable, and served websites. TUFTE is its
user-form topology engine. The engineering realization is the
[VICTOR application](../../../../README.md).

VICTOR publishes content as TUFTE-rendered HTML and runs no server. Serving belongs to the web-server, and interactive applications belong to TUFTE. A VICTOR build is static output that any host, such as Neocities, can serve. Source: Leader utterance `ni:///sha-256;zTkIU2KZqDYZMawL04SZI_zDCIXdCj-1dgTI5FCo7tQ` (2026-09-29).

The Leader does not love this backronym (utterance ni:///sha-256;NnNE0whzySmZeAzxKpuveYV3ympXdZpXgcDTujL6T-U, 2026-08-27); the expansion above is held provisionally and is to be replaced when the Leader accepts another. VICTOR's scope is to publish HTML from a TUFTE server: logic about websites belongs to TUFTE, and nothing FeltOS needs belongs in VICTOR (see `web-family-by-lineage-and-a-stable-local-port.json` in the web engineering section's requirements).
